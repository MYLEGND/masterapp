# Independent language evidence audit

The nine frozen native capability cases remain unaccepted. Their current receipts establish failed meaning selection before downstream execution; external responses cannot count as native execution.

The existing Founder curriculum contract accepts surface-grounded meaning nodes, relations, discourse references and transitions. Machine teaching proposals carry components and transitions, require an existing eligible semantic family, and do not carry arbitrary meaning graphs. No CoNLL-U, Universal Dependencies or AMR annotation importer was found. Fetching an annotated corpus as plain text does not map its annotations into governed reasoning evidence.

Independent candidate resources were inspected from primary publishers: [English Web Treebank](https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_English-EWT) and [Haitian Creole Adolphe treebank](https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Haitian_Creole-Adolphe). Their documentation describes annotation provenance and licensing. Neither is already a LEGEND semantic/operator curriculum. No corpus was downloaded, imported, promoted, or used to alter held-out examples.

A legitimate adapter would have to preserve source/version/sentence lineage and explicit surface grounding, map annotations to established semantic dimensions, and submit reviewable declarations through the existing admission authority. Syntactic relations alone do not establish arithmetic roles, causal validity, private-data authorization, or inference transitions. This audit does not claim such an adapter or a learned interpretation capability has been implemented.

A separate integration reproduction uses the pre-existing executor control of rate times quantity plus fee, with its original unseen numeric values, to distinguish language binding and rule retrieval defects from absent curriculum. It does not amend frozen questions, expected answers, thresholds, or the shared foundation.
